Room-only blur: foreground color preservation

This isolated fixture renders synthetic untonemapped color artwork and white reference patches over a flat background, a blurred room, and finally the clear-glass control compositor. The room is rendered separately; no reading state, storage or application scene is imported.

Eight cases exercise light/dark, DPR 1/2 and two sizes. Colored art interiors and white patches must remain exact RGBA matches. Background pixels must change, room cache must invalidate on view/size/theme changes, and the control pass must preserve all pixels outside its bounded capture region. This verifies this browser/GPU, not visual acceptance or cross-device performance.
